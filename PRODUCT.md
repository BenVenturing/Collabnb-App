# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creators seek well-matched property collaborations and manage applications, saved opportunities, and host conversations from a phone. Hosts discover creators, publish collaboration opportunities, and coordinate deliverables.

## Product Purpose

Collabnb connects hospitality hosts and content creators around clear, structured property collaborations. Success means users can discover, assess, apply for, and coordinate an opportunity without losing context between web and native.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, premium. The interface should feel like a considered travel tool, not a marketplace feed or a generic social app.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic Airbnb clones, flashy creator-economy social UI, dark-pattern marketplace urgency, decorative glass effects, and visual treatments that differ between web and native without a product reason.

## Design Principles

- One product contract: web data and business rules are authoritative; native presents the same decisions in a phone-native flow.
- Opportunity first: make compensation, deliverables, location, and fit easy to scan before asking for commitment.
- Calm coordination: messaging, applications, and status should reduce ambiguity rather than create feed-like noise.
- Familiar mobile behavior: navigation, search, filtering, and feedback should be immediately legible on iOS and Android.
- Honest states: loading, empty, error, and unavailable states explain what happened and how to proceed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, screen-reader labels for controls, touch targets of at least 44 points, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
